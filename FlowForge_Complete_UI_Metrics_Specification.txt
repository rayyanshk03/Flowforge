3. PAGE 02 · REAL-TIME DISRUPTION INTELLIGENCE (`/disruptions`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTIONS & METRICS:
- Disaster & Weather Radar: Real-time Open-Meteo weather hazard feed, GDELT geopolitical alerts, and AIS vessel queue density.
- Port Congestion Registry: Table of 120+ UN/LOCODE global ports with latitude, longitude, total berths, occupied berths, and calculated Congestion Score (0.0 - 1.0).

4. PAGE 03 · SCENARIO STUDIO & MULTI-MODEL INGESTION MODAL (`/api/v1/analyze`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTIONS & INPUT FORM:
1. Corridor Selection: Origin UN/LOCODE (e.g. INNSA), Destination UN/LOCODE (e.g. NLRTM).
2. Carrier & Mode: Carrier (`MAERSK`, `MSC`, `CMA_CGM`), Mode (`Ocean`, `Air`, `Truck`).
3. Cargo Specifications: Cargo Weight (MT), Cargo Quantity (Units), Declared Value ($ USD).
4. Vendor & IncoTerms: Vendor Name, Fulfill Via, IncoTerms (`FOB`, `CIF`, `DDP`).
5. Baseline Constraints: Baseline ETA (Hours), Deadline Buffer (Hours).
Action: Click `RUN FLOWFORGE ANALYSIS →` to post to `POST /api/v1/analyze` (triggers 4 ML models + 10,000 Monte Carlo simulation runs).
